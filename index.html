💰 Project grants: the big money

ProjectSB / any chapter

EPICS in IEEE

up to US$10,000

Funds engineering projects for local communities (schools, NGOs, villages). Students design, build and deploy the solution, with mentorship and resources.

  • Student branches can apply: at least one IEEE member, and the team must include students.
  • Two calls a year: 1 Mar–1 May and 1 Sep–1 Oct.
  • Funded teams can claim up to US$1,000 to travel and present at IEEE conferences.

IEEE Tech4Good (SIGHT)

US$2,500 – 10,000

Humanitarian-technology projects run by SIGHT groups. In 2026, proposals were due by 15 March.

  • Students can be on the team but can't be the primary applicant, so a faculty member or IEEE professional leads.
  • Forming a SIGHT group under our SB unlocks this and smaller SIGHT funds.

07 · How SUAS IEEE runs

Always-on committees

Event teams form for each event. Between events, every member also belongs to one always-on committee whose job is to grow SUAS IEEE: reporting, funding, members, collaborations, projects and visibility. The quiet months are when we build.

📋

vTools & Compliance Desk

Goal: perfect IEEE records
  • Report every event on vTools within 48 hours, with photos and attendance
  • Keep officer reporting current for the SB and all chapters
  • File the SB Annual Plan before 1 Feb
  • Check OU Analytics every month
KPI: 100% of events reported · 4+ per unit per year

Grants & Funding

Goal: multiple funded proposals per year

08 · The playbook

How we win these

Report everything on vTools

Reviewers check our track record there. Some Societies, like SMC, only count events reported on vTools.

Get the Section on our side

Many grants need Section endorsement. Money is routed through approved IEEE accounts, never personal ones.

Partner with a real community

Every winning project above named an NGO, school or panchayat. Find the partner first, then design the tech.

Apply before, report after

Most funds don't cover past events and need receipts plus a final report. A late report blocks next year's funding.

One unit, its own funders

SB, CS, WIE and AI/ML each apply to their own sources, so we don't compete with ourselves.

Use the free training

EPICS offers a proposal-writing course and budgeting videos. Watch them before writing.
